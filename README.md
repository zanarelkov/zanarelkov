## ▍About

<small>I'm a self-taught security practitioner who learns by building: I break systems to understand how they fail, then harden them so they hold up. My foundation is systems and network administration, and my experience comes from my homelab, CTFs with OwlSec and the products I build.</small>

<br>

## ▍Pentest & Red Team

<small>Offensive security is where I spend most of my time, learned hands-on through lab environments, vulnerable machines and CTFs.</small>

**Web & network testing**<br>
<small>Attack surface mapping, service enumeration and vulnerability discovery with Burp Suite, Nmap and Metasploit.</small>

**Exploit development**<br>
<small>Proof-of-concept exploits and low-level vulnerability research in Python, Rust and Assembly.</small>

**Post-exploitation**<br>
<small>Privilege escalation, and understanding what an attacker leaves behind for defenders to find.</small>

**Reporting**<br>
<small>Clear findings with impact and remediation, written in SysReptor.</small>

<br>

## ▍Engineering & Defense

**Detection & response**<br>
<small>Log analysis and alert investigation with Wazuh, Splunk and ELK, plus network monitoring with Suricata, Snort and Zeek.</small>

**Systems & networking**<br>
<small>Linux and network administration, virtualization, containers and reverse proxies.</small>

**Hardening**<br>
<small>Secure configuration, network segmentation and least privilege.</small>

**Development**<br>
<small>Backend, APIs and automation, with code analysis through SonarQube, Qodana and Snyk.</small>

<br>

## ▍Homelab & Projects

<small>My homelab is where most of my learning happens: I build it, break it and monitor it.</small>

**Lab**<br>
<small>Proxmox virtualization with segmented networks, self-hosted services behind reverse proxies, my own PKI, and remote access through NetBird only. Wazuh, ELK and network sensors monitor all of it.</small>

**Projects**<br>
<small>Product work at <a href="https://github.com/MiahouTeam">Miahou</a> and <a href="https://vybe.moe">Vybe</a>, plus private personal projects and automation tooling. Everything follows security-first design.</small>
